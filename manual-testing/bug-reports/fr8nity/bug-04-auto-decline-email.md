# FR8_P3_034 — Auto-Decline Email Not Received

## Bug Type
Functional

## Severity
Minor

## Priority
Medium

## Environment
Desktop / Web

## Description

The email notification expected when a meeting request is automatically declined was not received.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Navigate to the meeting request functionality.
2. Create or configure a meeting request that results in an automatic decline.
3. Complete the required actions to trigger the auto-decline condition.
4. Check the recipient's registered email inbox.
5. Verify whether the expected auto-decline email is received.

## Expected Result

When a meeting request is automatically declined, the appropriate email notification should be sent to the relevant user.

## Actual Result

The expected auto-decline email was not received.

## Impact

Missing email notifications can prevent users from being informed about the outcome of their meeting requests and may cause confusion regarding the request status.

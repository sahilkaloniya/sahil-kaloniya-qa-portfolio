# SD_026 — Incorrect Placement of "Notify Me When Available" Action

## Bug Type
Functional

## Severity
Major

## Priority
High

## Environment
Desktop / Web

## Description

The "Notify me when available" action was displayed under the "Add to cart" button instead of being associated with the "Sold out" state.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Navigate to the affected product page.
2. Observe the product availability state.
3. Locate the "Notify me when available" action.
4. Check its placement relative to the product's availability and purchase actions.

## Expected Result

The "Notify me when available" action should appear under the "Sold out" state, where it is relevant to the product's availability.

## Actual Result

The "Notify me when available" button appeared under the "Add to cart" button.

## Impact

Incorrect placement of an availability-related action can confuse users and make the purchase/notification flow unclear.
